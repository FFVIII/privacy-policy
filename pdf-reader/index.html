PDF Reader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Data Collection

PDF Reader does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your PDFs, bookmarks, highlights, notes, and reading progress are stored locally on your device only. No account or sign-in is required.

Camera & Photos

The Scan tool uses your camera to capture document pages directly into a PDF. The Image → PDF tool lets you pick photos to convert into a PDF. Both happen entirely on your device — no image ever leaves your device through these features.

Text Recognition (OCR)

Scanned pages and the PDF → Text tool use Apple's on-device Vision framework to recognize text. This processing happens entirely on your device; no scanned content is sent to any server.

AI Features (Coming Soon)

The app has placeholders for an AI Explain feature and an AI note-organizing feature. These are not active in the current version and do not make any network request. Once enabled in a future update, using them will send the text you select, or your notes, to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response. This policy will be updated when that feature ships.

Third-Party Services

This app does not currently use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and makes no network requests. The only third-party service it will ever contact is Anthropic's Claude API, and only if you use the AI features described above once they are enabled.

Permissions

This app requests access to your camera (to scan documents) and your photo library (to import images as a PDF). It does not request access to your microphone, location, or contacts.

PDF Reader — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年9月3日

数据收集

PDF Reader 不收集、存储或传输任何个人数据。您的 PDF 文件、书签、高亮、笔记和阅读进度均仅存储在您的设备本地，无需注册或登录账号。

相机与照片

扫描功能会使用相机将文档页面直接拍摄并生成 PDF。「图片转 PDF」工具可选择照片转换为 PDF。这两项功能均完全在您的设备本地完成，不会通过这些功能上传任何图片。

文字识别（OCR）

扫描页面和「PDF 转文本」工具使用苹果设备端的 Vision 框架进行文字识别，全部处理均在您的设备本地完成，不会将扫描内容发送至任何服务器。

AI 功能（即将推出）

应用中已经预留了 AI 解释和 AI 笔记整理功能的入口，当前版本尚未启用，不会产生任何网络请求。未来版本启用后，使用这些功能会将您选中的文字或笔记发送至 Anthropic 的 Claude API 以生成结果。届时本政策会同步更新。

第三方服务

本应用目前不使用任何第三方分析、广告或追踪服务，也不会发起任何网络请求。未来唯一可能访问的第三方服务是 Anthropic 的 Claude API，且仅在您启用并使用上述 AI 功能时才会发生。

权限

本应用会请求访问您的相机（用于扫描文档）和照片库（用于将图片导入为 PDF）。本应用不会请求访问您的麦克风、位置或通讯录。
