Life Tips — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Data Collection

Life Tips does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your emergency numbers, contacts, insurance policies, and custom zones are stored locally on your device only. No account or sign-in is required.

Making Calls and Sending Messages

When you tap a phone number, the app opens your device's native dialer using the standard tel: URL scheme. When you send an SOS message, it opens your native messaging app using the sms: URL scheme. Life Tips does not make calls or send messages itself, and does not have access to your call history or messages.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking services.

Permissions

Life Tips does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or notifications. No special permissions are required to use the app.

Contact

If you have any questions, please contact: fangvsyu@gmail.com

Life Tips — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年8月15日

数据收集

Life Tips 不收集、存储或传输任何个人数据。您的急救号码、紧急联系人、保单信息和自定义板块均仅存储在您的设备本地，无需注册或登录账号。

拨打电话与发送消息

当您点击电话号码时，应用通过系统标准的 tel: 协议打开设备自带的拨号界面。当您发送求救短信时，通过 sms: 协议打开系统自带的短信应用。Life Tips 本身不拨打电话，也不发送消息，无法访问您的通话记录或短信内容。

第三方服务

本应用不使用任何第三方分析、广告、崩溃统计或追踪服务。

权限

Life Tips 不会请求访问您的相机、麦克风、位置、通讯录或通知权限。使用本应用无需任何特殊权限。

联系方式

如有任何疑问，请联系：fangvsyu@gmail.com
