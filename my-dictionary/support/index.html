How do I add a word?

Use Capture to quickly jot down a word, record a voice note, or snap a photo — it goes to your Inbox, where you can turn it into a full flashcard with a definition, IPA, part of speech, and example sentence.

What are the games for?

Hangman, Quiz, and Word Match all pull from your own library so practice always uses words you're actually learning — pick "Due for Review" to focus on what's scheduled today, or "All Words" for free practice.

How does review scheduling work?

My Dictionary uses spaced repetition (SRS): words you know well come back less often, words you struggle with come back sooner. The Library's due count and Review screen always reflect what's actually due today.

What do the AI-powered features need?

Generating word details, quizzes, chat practice, and identifying words from a photo all use an AI service and need a connection to work. Everything else — your library, capture, and games — works fully offline.

How do I import or export my words?

Go to Settings → Export/Import to save your library to a file, or use the Web Import tool to add words (with photos) from a browser on another device on the same WiFi network.

如何添加单词？

使用「随手记录」快速记下一个单词、录一段语音，或拍张照片——它会进入收件箱，您可以在那里把它整理成包含释义、音标、词性和例句的完整卡片。

游戏是做什么用的？

Hangman、测验和连连看都取材自您自己的词库，练习内容始终是您正在学习的单词——选择「待复习」专注于今天该复习的内容，或选择「全部单词」自由练习。

复习计划是如何安排的？

My Dictionary 使用间隔重复（SRS）：您掌握得好的单词会更久才出现，掌握不牢的单词会更快出现。词库的待复习数量和复习页面始终反映当天真实需要复习的内容。

AI 相关功能需要什么条件？

生成单词详情、测验题目、聊天练习，以及从照片识别单词，都需要联网使用 AI 服务。其余功能——词库、随手记录和游戏——完全可以离线使用。

如何导入或导出我的单词？

进入设置 → 导出/导入，可以将词库保存为文件；也可以使用「网页导入」工具，从同一 WiFi 网络下的另一台设备的浏览器中添加单词（含照片）。
