What's the difference between Simple, Plan, and Budget mode?

Simple is a plain checklist. Plan lets you assign a category and shelf/aisle to each item and estimate how long it will last, so it moves straight into your inventory once you check it off. Budget tracks quantity and price as you shop, with a running total.

How do restock reminders work?

Turn on Restock reminder in Settings and choose how many days ahead you want to be notified. Items running low show up in the Alerts tab and can be added back to your list with one tap.

Can I customize categories and shelf order?

Yes. Go to Settings → Product categories or Aisle order to add, rename, or reorder them to match how your kitchen or pantry is actually laid out. Your shopping list and inventory both group items the same way.

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Backup & export to save your shopping list, inventory, categories, and settings to a spreadsheet file. Use Import & restore to load a previously exported file.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All of your data is stored on your device, and the app works fully without an internet connection.

简单、计划、记账模式有什么区别？

简单模式是一个纯清单，勾选即可。计划模式可以为每件商品指定分类和货架/区域，并估算它能用多久，勾选后会直接进入库存。记账模式在购物过程中记录数量和价格，并自动汇总总价。

补货提醒是如何工作的？

在设置中打开补货提醒，并选择提前几天提醒。库存不足的商品会出现在「提醒」页面，一键即可重新加入购物清单。

可以自定义分类和货架顺序吗？

可以。进入设置 → 商品分类 或 货架顺序，添加、重命名或调整顺序，让它符合您厨房或储藏室的实际布局。购物清单和库存会按相同的方式分组。

如何备份我的数据？

进入设置 → 备份导出，即可将购物清单、库存、分类和设置导出为表格文件。使用「导入恢复」可以载入此前导出的文件。

需要联网使用吗？

不需要。所有数据都存储在您的设备上，应用在完全离线的情况下也能正常使用。
