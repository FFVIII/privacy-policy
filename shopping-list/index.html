Shopping List — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Data Collection

Shopping List does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your shopping lists, inventory, categories, and settings are stored locally on your device only. No account or sign-in is required.

Notifications

If you enable restock reminders, the app schedules local notifications directly on your device to let you know when items are running low. This is handled entirely on-device and does not involve any server.

Backup & Export

You may export your data to a spreadsheet file, or import a previously exported file, using your device's native share sheet. This only happens when you choose to do so — your data is never uploaded automatically.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Permissions

This app requests permission to send local notifications for restock reminders. It also declares microphone and speech-recognition permissions for an optional voice-input feature that is not enabled in the current version. It does not request access to your camera, location, or contacts.

Contact

If you have any questions, please contact: fangvsyu@gmail.com

购物清单 — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年7月13日

数据收集

购物清单不收集、存储或传输任何个人数据。您的购物清单、库存、分类和设置均仅存储在您的设备本地，无需注册或登录账号。

通知

如果您开启补货提醒，应用会在您的设备上直接安排本地通知，提醒您库存不足的商品。此功能完全在设备端完成，不涉及任何服务器。

备份与导出

您可以通过设备自带的分享功能，将数据导出为表格文件，或导入此前导出的文件。此操作仅在您主动选择时才会发生，您的数据不会被自动上传。

第三方服务

本应用不使用任何第三方分析、广告或追踪服务。

权限

本应用请求发送本地通知的权限，用于补货提醒。此外，应用中还声明了麦克风与语音识别权限，用于当前版本尚未启用的语音输入功能。本应用不会请求访问您的相机、位置或通讯录。

联系方式

如有任何疑问，请联系：fangvsyu@gmail.com
