How does the wheel work?

Enter a location or use your current position, then tap Search. The wheel loads nearby restaurants and you spin to get a pick. If you don't like the result, tap Spin Again — the previous pick won't appear in the same session.

What is the Cuisine Wheel?

The Cuisine Wheel lets you spin for a food category first — Chinese, Japanese, Italian, and so on — then shows you matching restaurants nearby. Use it when you know you want something specific but can't decide which cuisine.

How do I build a Custom Wheel?

Go to the Custom Wheel tab and add any restaurants you like. You can add places from your spin history, or type in any name manually. Once you've built your list, spin it the same way as the main wheel.

Can I filter out cuisines I don't want?

Yes. Open Settings and tap Excluded Cuisines to select any categories you want the wheel to skip. You can also set a minimum rating so only higher-rated restaurants appear.

Where is my data stored?

All your favorites, spin history, custom wheel items, and settings are saved on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and no account is needed.

The app can't find restaurants near me — what should I check?

Make sure Location access is enabled for Meal Wheel in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Also check that you have an internet connection, as restaurant search requires it.

转盘是怎么运作的？

输入地点或使用当前位置，点击搜索。转盘会加载附近的餐厅，旋转后随机选出一家。如果不满意，点击「再转一次」——本次会话中，已出现过的结果不会重复。

菜系转盘是什么？

菜系转盘让您先旋转选出一个菜系——中餐、日料、意大利菜等——再显示附近符合该菜系的餐厅。适合已经有大方向、但还没决定吃哪种菜的时候使用。

如何创建自定义转盘？

进入「自定义转盘」页面，添加您喜欢的餐厅。可以从转盘历史记录中添加，也可以手动输入任意名称。建好列表后，旋转方式与主转盘相同。

可以过滤掉不想要的菜系吗？

可以。打开设置，点击「排除菜系」选择不想出现的类型。您还可以设置最低评分，只显示评分较高的餐厅。

我的数据存在哪里？

所有收藏、转盘记录、自定义转盘内容和设置均保存在您的设备上，不会上传至任何服务器，也无需注册账号。

应用找不到附近的餐厅，怎么办？

请确认已在 iPhone 的「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 定位服务」中为 Meal Wheel 开启位置权限。同时确保设备已连接网络，餐厅搜索功能需要联网使用。
