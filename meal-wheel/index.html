Meal Wheel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Data Collection

Meal Wheel does not collect or transmit any personal data to its own servers. Your favorites, spin history, custom wheel items, and settings are stored locally on your device only. No account or sign-in is required.

Location

When you search for nearby restaurants, the app uses your device's location to find results in your area. Your location is sent directly to Google Places API to perform the search — it is not stored by the app or sent anywhere else.

Third-Party Services

This app uses the Google Places API to search for restaurants. When you perform a search, your approximate location and search query are sent to Google. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle this data. The app does not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Permissions

The app requests access to your device location to find nearby restaurants. Location is only used when you initiate a search and is never stored or shared beyond what is described above.

Meal Wheel — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年8月18日

数据收集

Meal Wheel 不向自有服务器收集或传输任何个人数据。您的收藏夹、转盘历史记录、自定义转盘内容和设置均仅存储在您的设备本地，无需注册或登录账号。

位置信息

当您搜索附近餐厅时，应用会使用您设备的位置来查找附近的结果。您的位置信息将直接发送给 Google Places API 用于执行搜索——应用本身不存储此信息，也不会将其发送至其他地方。

第三方服务

本应用使用 Google Places API 搜索餐厅。执行搜索时，您的大致位置和搜索词会发送给 Google。请参阅 Google 隐私政策 了解其如何处理这些数据。本应用不使用任何分析、广告或崩溃统计服务。

权限

本应用请求访问您设备位置的权限，用于查找附近的餐厅。位置信息仅在您主动发起搜索时使用，不会被存储或以超出上述描述的方式共享。

联系方式
